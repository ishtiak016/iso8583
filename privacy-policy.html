ISO8583 Lab Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Your analysis stays local

ISO8583 Lab processes the message, bitmap and EMV/TLV data you enter on your device. The app has no account system and does not send your entered payment-message content to the developer. Avoid entering live PAN, PIN, Track 2, keys or other confidential production data.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner and interstitial advertisements, and Google User Messaging Platform to collect privacy choices where required. Google may process IP address, device and account identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and approximate location derived from IP for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. Available privacy choices can be reopened from the app home screen.

Storage and permissions

The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or storage permissions. Theme preference and ad-frequency counters are stored locally on the device.

Children

This technical payment-message utility is not designed for children. The publisher's Google Play target-audience declaration describes its intended audience.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. For privacy questions, use the developer contact details on the app's Google Play listing.
